◆ Non-Regular Crystal From Pieces ◆

Photograph your flat 2D sheet-material off-cuts → fold them (virtually) into a closed 3D crystal.
1. Input  
Standard: light pieces on a dark background, non-overlapping, photographed straight-on, outside surface of each piece facing the camera.
2. Options
Complexity (contour detail): simple complex
Seed:  
Every setting produces a crystal: if the pieces cannot close exactly, the edge lengths are adjusted as little as possible and the adjustment is reported below.
