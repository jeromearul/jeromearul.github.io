jerome arul is a designer from singapore,
and works at the rhode island school of design.

he began playing the drums at age 11, under the tutelage of dan-djuma legos, in geneva switzerland.
at age 16, he painted with the dutch master frederik van hasselt whilst hiking through the yunnan province.

after a stint as an engineer in the army, his work evolved from street art to industrial design.
his first job was designing and building an off-road vehicle in nairobi kenya.

after working on superpedestrian's copenhagen wheel, he started an underground fabrication shop called proto roboto.

today, he works on anything from robotics to kinetic art, inspired by geometry and nature.

ecozoom, 2015
charcoal cookstove

ecozoom is a B Corp that makes portable stoves powered by wood and charcoal.
improved cookstoves significantly reduce indoor air pollution and use fuel efficiently.
ecozoom cookstoves have been deployed by the World Food Programme, the International Committee of the Red Cross and Relief International, through programs with UNICEF and UNFCCC.

design: d. sierra
