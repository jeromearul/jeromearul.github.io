how to construct A and B quanta modules


A regular tetrahedron can be made from quanta A modules.
There are right and left handed versions.
24 of these make up the volume of the tetrahedron

Quanta B modules can be made from dividing the Octahedron
and also come in right and left handed versions.
They are the same volume as the A modules.

fuller always derives the quanta B modules from the octahedron,
though it can just as easily be derived from the tetrahedron as well.
notice that the centroid of the tetrahedron lies at exactly a 1/4 of it's altitude.
this is a progression from the equilateral triangle, who's centroid is exactly a 1/3 of it's altitude.
