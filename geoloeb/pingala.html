Acharya Pingala (c. 300 BCE) was an ancient poet and mathematician.
A pioneer in combinatorics, he was interested in constructing
poems of a certain length, with a certain number of syllables.

He listed possible forms of an n-syllable meter
and created a recursive algorithm to generate combinations
of long and short syllables.
